Hexpaws! Support

Need help? Email joshuacai2701@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and what happened.

I bought the Wave Pass but don't have it

Open the Shop and tap Restore purchases at the bottom of the Wave Pass page. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account you bought it with.

I got a new phone. Is my progress saved?

Progress is saved on your phone only. Purchases always come back with Restore purchases.

Ads

Ads appear only when you choose a second chance, and once every few runs. To change ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland), go to Settings → Ad privacy in the game.

Hexpaws! Privacy Policy

Effective: November 9, 2026 · Developer: Joshua Cai

Hexpaws! is a game for iPhone. This policy explains what information the game and the services it uses handle. In short: we don't run any servers, there's no account, and we never sell your data. Ads are shown by Google AdMob, and purchases go through Apple, with RevenueCat checking them.

Information the game keeps on your phone

Your progress is saved only on your device and is never sent to us. This includes waves reached, coins, champions, settings and the Wave Pass. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads only in two places: an optional ad you choose to watch for a second chance after a loss, and a short ad every few runs. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier, but only if you allow tracking when the game asks (Apple's "Allow Tracking" prompt). You can change this any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking;
  • device and app information, such as the model, OS version and language;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • how you interact with ads.

Players in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form. You can change your choice any time in the game: Settings → Ad privacy. Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy, and how Google uses data from apps that use its services is at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

The optional Wave Pass is bought through Apple's App Store. We never see your payment details. RevenueCat processes the purchase receipt so the game knows what you own and can restore it on a new phone. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history for this game, and basic device information. Their privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Children

Hexpaws! is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Decline tracking: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Change ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): in the game, Settings → Ad privacy.
  • Delete everything on your device: delete the app.
  • Questions or data requests: email us (below). Because we keep nothing ourselves, requests about ad or purchase data are passed to Google or RevenueCat.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
